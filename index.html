15 floors. 108,000 items. One grand opening.
Carry a SINGLE can at the start. Earn 1¢ for a bottom-shelf item, 2¢ for the middle shelf, and 3¢ for the very top. Collect your wages at the PAY WINDOW, then visit the mustached shopkeeper to buy upgrades. Eventually let conveyor belts and robots do all the work.

Move: WASD · Hold E: interact · Mouse or arrows: look · Q: shopkeeper (nearby) · R: elevator (nearby) · B: extend conveyor · Space: jump (upgrade) · L: place ladder · Esc: pause
